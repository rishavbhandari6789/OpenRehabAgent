# Safety and Limitations

OpenRehabAgent is a research prototype. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

## Intended use

- research demonstration
- multi-agent software architecture exploration
- AI workflow experimentation
- education and reproducible software study

## Not intended for

- diagnosis
- treatment
- clinical decision-making
- patient-specific physiotherapy
- emergency use

## Current limitations

1. Synthetic pose data only.
2. No clinical dataset.
3. Pain scores are heuristic.
4. Exercise recommendations are simplified.
5. Safety rules are illustrative.
6. No real patient validation.
7. LLM explanation layer is template-based by default.

## Responsible AI controls

- audit trail for agent decisions
- explicit disclaimer in explanations
- supervisor safety filtering
- transparent rule-based logic
- no clinical claims
- deterministic testing support

## Future work

- clinician-reviewed safety rules
- real video pose-estimation adapter
- stronger evaluation dataset
- fairness and bias review
- model cards
- deployment risk assessment
